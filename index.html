TeamTunes is the wildly addictive music-guessing party game that brings the heat when you've got three or more players ready to rock. The rules

How to play

Easy to learn in under a minute. Hard to put down once you start.

1

Split into teams

Gather at least 3 players and divide into teams. Play with a small group of friends or scale up to a big party — the more the merrier.

2

Pick a song

The active team picks any song from their favorite music service. They choose whether the others must guess the song, artist, or album. Pick strategically — songs the other teams might know (but can't quite name).

3

Guess!

The music plays — and the race is on. The first team to shout the correct answer wins 2 points. The picking team earns 1 point for a successful pick. If nobody guesses, the picking team loses a point.

4

Pass & repeat

The phone passes to the next team and it's their turn to pick. After all teams have gone, the round ends and scores are shown. Keep playing until your win condition is met.

Smart scoring

Strategy makes it interesting

Picking an obscure deep cut nobody knows might feel safe — but it'll cost you a point if nobody guesses. Pick something too obvious and the other team scoops the 2 points. Stay sharp.
